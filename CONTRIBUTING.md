# Contributing

The CrocoSign repositories are a research release: the code, data pipeline and models behind the paper "A Polish Sign Language to Text Dataset and Benchmark". We maintain them on a best-effort basis and do not plan new features.

- **Issues** are welcome for bugs, reproduction problems and questions about the data or the results. Include the command you ran, the error, and your environment.
- **Pull requests** are welcome for bug fixes and documentation corrections. Please open an issue before larger changes, so we can tell you whether they fit the release.
- **Security problems** go through private reporting, not issues (see `SECURITY.md`).
- **Data**: the corpus is non-commercial only. Requests to remove a person's recordings follow the procedure in the `pjm-data` README, not public issues.
